Challenge Room

Put any plan on the table and let four sharp minds attack it. Each gives its strongest objection, the assumption you haven't proved, and the cheapest experiment that could change your mind. "The reasoning holds" is a real possible answer.

The plan on the table

Tabby: auto-chasing house bills, €2 per house per month

Pilot with 4 student houses for two weeks · from your notes

Your call

AVA records what you decided and why, next to the evidence. The challenge becomes part of the idea's history in Decision Replay.

What's demo

  • The critics' words are scripted for the fictional "Tabby" plan.
  • Nothing is saved anywhere.

What would be real

  • Critiques bound to your actual note (exact ID and version).
  • Every objection must name the note it's reacting to.
  • Your verdict is stored as a decision event.

How it plugs into AVA

An explicit "Challenge this" on a Note or plan. It's a source-bound review, not a generic skeptic prompt; the persona already challenges assumptions. It feeds Decision Replay.

Grounding

Role-based multi-agent critique (persona debate, COLING 2025) and the premortem technique (Gary Klein). It's a thinking aid, not a truth score.
