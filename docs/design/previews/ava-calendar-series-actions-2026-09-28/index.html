What AVA says by voice

The same actions work by voice. She asks before removing, and her answers stay short and honest.

you: "remove the duplicate Monday lecture""There are two Software Design lectures at 10 on Mondays. Remove the one added on 28 Sep? It has already started, so its past ones will be hidden too."
you: "yes""Done. The original stays. You can undo it in Calendar."
you: "I dropped Maths tutorials, stop them""Maths tutorial now ends after Mon 28 Sep. The past ones and what you recorded stay. You can undo it in Calendar."
you: "end Maths tutorials from tomorrow""I can't end it from tomorrow: the 12 Oct tutorial was moved to Saturday and already happened, so ending would hide it. Want me to try ending it from 19 Oct? I'll check that one first."
you: "set the lecture to 10 o'clock" (it already is)"It's already at 10. Nothing to change."

Decisions for you

  • Where do the actions live?Proposed: in the sheet you get when you tap an event, under "Whole series". The series editor stays for changing times and details.
  • How careful is Remove for a series that has already started?Proposed: one confirmation that says plainly the past ones will be hidden too. No typing the name.
  • How long can you undo?Proposed: an Undo button right after, for about 10 seconds, and AVA can point you to it. A "recently removed" list could come later.

What's demo

  • Events, dates and names are invented.
  • Buttons change only this page; nothing is saved.

What would be real

  • Remove hides a series; nothing is deleted, and Undo brings it back.
  • End keeps every past occurrence and what you recorded.
  • Specific reasons when something can't be done.

Honest limits

  • AVA can't bring a removed series back by voice yet; Undo is in Calendar.
  • Event-linked notifications aren't built yet, so there are no reminders to clean up.
