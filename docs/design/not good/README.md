# Rejected surfaces — Niko's feedback

Recorded 27 September 2026. The three original screenshots in this folder are Niko's references; they are not changed or republished by this work.

## What Niko explicitly rejected

- The current cards and backgrounds: he wants four strong alternatives to compare before agreeing a direction.
- The Calendar needs a substantially better design.
- The Notes tab feels poor and needs a rethink.
- Earlier feedback still matters: AVA should feel alive, the metallic material is appealing, Home/Voice should be sparse, and Home should fit its viewport rather than become a scrolling dashboard.

## What the screenshots show (our interpretation, not extra requirements from Niko)

| Reference | Visible issue | Design response to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screenshot 2026-09-26 234957.png | Several large, equally weighted blue agenda panels | Time-proportional events and a quieter agenda rail; four deliberately different card structures |
| Screenshot 2026-09-26 235024.png | A prominent outlined title input inside a surrounding form | A title-first editor, with secondary scheduling fields beneath it |
| Screenshot 2026-09-26 235106.png | A strong rectangular text-entry boundary inside another rounded container | Reduce nested framing; focus indication remains visible, but need not look like another permanent card |

These are observations to validate with Niko, not claims that he rejected every border, every blue accent, or the overall navigation layout.
