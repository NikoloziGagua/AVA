Future You

"If I do X now, where does it put me in 2–5 years?" AVA lays out a few branching paths from the goals you confirm: what each would ask of you, what must be true, and the early signs worth watching. Then you can talk it through with a version of yourself at 26.

What's demo

  • The branches, milestones and the "26" conversation are scripted.
  • "Fit" is a toy score of overlap with the goals you ticked.

What would be real

  • Branches built only from goals you explicitly confirm; never inferred into memory.
  • Each milestone links to what you'd need (skills, money, time).
  • No probabilities presented as forecasts.

How it plugs into AVA

It reads your confirmed goals and projects in Notes, and reviews them at goal check-ins. The conversation is an ordinary chat with a clearly labelled persona. Nothing it says is saved as fact about you.

Grounding

MIT Media Lab's Future You: short chats with an AI future self reduced anxiety and increased "future self-continuity" in a controlled study. The authors are explicit: it creates plausible scenarios and cannot predict your life.
