How she remembers

Three layers, so she doesn't have to re-read everything you've ever said. A shorter start is one way to make her faster; we'd measure it before promising a number.

1
This chapter, word for word

The recent turns of what you're talking about now. Bounded, so it never grows forever.

2
Chapter summaries

A short summary written when a chapter ends, linked to its messages. Looked up only when it's relevant.

3
What I'm carrying forward

Decisions, promises and preferences, each with where it came from. This is what she knows at the start of every call.

What I'm carrying forward

proposed sheet

A small sheet you can open any time. Each item says whether you said it or AVA guessed, and links to where it came from. Confirm a guess, or remove anything that's wrong.

Decisions for you

  • Keep old chats under History, searchable?Proposed: yes. Nothing is deleted or merged; today's chats become chapters.
  • When should a new chapter start by itself?Proposed: after about 6 hours away, or whenever you say "new topic".
  • Should ideas AVA starts appear in the same timeline?Proposed: yes, as their own short chapter you can answer or ignore.
  • Voice and typing together?Proposed: one timeline. Spoken turns get a small voice mark.
  • When you switch project, should AVA use only that project?Proposed: yes. Other projects stay out of that chapter unless you ask.

What's demo

  • All conversation text, names and times are invented.
  • The talk button doesn't record anything.
  • The layer highlights explain the idea; they aren't a real trace.

What would be real

  • One Talk entry that always resumes, for both voice and typing.
  • Chapters are today's conversations, created automatically.
  • A compact start-of-call brief, plus look-ups on demand.
  • A carrying-forward sheet you can correct.

Honest limits

  • An iPhone web app can't listen for a wake word all the time. "Always there" means one tap from anywhere.
  • AVA only answers while your PC runs her.
  • Summaries can miss things, so each one links to the real messages.

If you like it: possible steps

1. One timeline over today's conversations, with automatic chapters (UI only).
2. Chapter summaries and the start-of-call brief, sized by a benchmark.
3. The carrying-forward sheet with corrections.
Each step gets your OK and a mockup first. Nothing here is built.
