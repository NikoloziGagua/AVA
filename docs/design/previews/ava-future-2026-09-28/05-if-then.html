If-Then Nudges

Not "remind me at 6", but "when I finish uni, remind me to buy printer paper." You say it naturally, and AVA shows exactly what will trigger it. Every trigger says honestly whether it's time-based or something you actually do.

What's demo

  • The day, times and saved nudges are simulated in this page only.
  • The location trigger is shown as unavailable on purpose.

What would be real

  • Time-based triggers use AVA's reminders and calendar.
  • Observed triggers fire only when you tell AVA (voice, chat or a tap).
  • Conditions are checked against what you've recorded, never guessed.

How it plugs into AVA

It extends one-off reminders (contract i4) with calendar events as triggers, and adds "I'm done" as an explicit observed event. The label distinguishes "the calendar says it ended" from "you said you left".

Grounding

If-then plans ("implementation intentions") are among the best-evidenced ways to follow through: a meta-analysis of 94 studies (Gollwitzer & Sheeran) found a medium-to-large effect on goal attainment (d≈0.65).
