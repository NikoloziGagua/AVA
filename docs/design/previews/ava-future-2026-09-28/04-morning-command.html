Morning Command Center

Wake up to what matters from overnight, say your plan out loud ("gym at 10, build AVA auth, lecture at 3, run at 6"), and AVA turns it into a day with gentle nudges. In the evening it asks how it went. It never assumes you did something just because it was planned.

What's demo

  • The overnight brief, the spoken plan and the times are scripted.

What would be real

  • Your spoken plan → optional calendar blocks you confirm.
  • Nudges as reminders.
  • The evening review writes a short journal line per project. Planned, done and deferred are stored separately.

How it plugs into AVA

This is M4, "life rhythm": voice plus the calendar tools plus one-off reminders already exist. The overnight brief reuses the "AVA starts a conversation" pipeline, with the same limits: quiet hours, sources shown, off until you turn it on.

Grounding

OpenAI's ChatGPT Pulse did overnight personal briefs, and was later folded into scheduled tasks (reported June 2026). The lesson: proactive briefs are loved, but they must stay scheduled and under your control.
