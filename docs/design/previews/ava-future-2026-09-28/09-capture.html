Where should this go?

Tap to speak · tap again to stop

One tap from anywhere. AVA appears in the phone's share sheet (a PWA share target). On iPhone, a Shortcut ("Hey Siri, tell AVA…") covers voice.
Filed where it belongs. AVA suggests a project folder and explains why. If it's unsure, it asks, and "Inbox" is always an option.
An exact receipt. "Saved to Tabby / Research", with Undo and a link back to the original page.
Offline-safe. With no signal, it's kept on the phone and sent once when you're back. A retry never creates a duplicate note.
Feeds everything else. Captures are what "AVA starts a conversation" and Decision Replay are built from.

What's demo

  • The article, folder suggestions and transcript are scripted.
  • This phone is a drawing, not the real share sheet.

What would be real

  • A PWA share target plus an iOS Shortcut.
  • Folder suggestions from your real Notes folders.
  • Idempotent saves with a receipt ID; voice memos transcribed by AVA's existing dictation.

How it plugs into AVA

This is M1 in the plan (capture anything). Notes, folders, dictation and push receipts already exist. The new part is the share target and the "file it for me" step, verified on your iPhone.

Why for you

You note things down constantly, and ideas die in camera rolls and chat histories. Capture is the foundation every other concept here feeds on.
