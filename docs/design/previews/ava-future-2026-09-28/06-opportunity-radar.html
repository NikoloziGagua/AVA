Opportunity Radar

AVA watches a few sources you pick (hackathon lists, fellowships, job boards) and pings you only when something truly matches your goals. It always says why it matched, when it's due, and where it came from.

Real examples, as read on 28 Sep 2026 (check the official pages)

  • Nebius × NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon: online, deadline listed as 30 Oct 2026, requires Nebius cloud or tokens. dev.to roundup #18
  • Verci Fellowship for young builders: deadline listed as 1 Oct 2026. same roundup
  • RevenueCat Shipaton 2026: build window 1 Aug – 30 Sep, submissions due 1 Oct 2026. dev.to roundup #13

Read from third-party roundups, not the organisers' own pages. Dates and rules must be checked at the official source before acting. The real radar would do that check itself.

What's demo

  • Every opportunity on the radar is invented.
  • The fit scores are illustrative.

What would be real

  • A few sources you add explicitly, each checked for changes (like changedetection.io, Apache-2.0).
  • Matches explained against goals you confirmed.
  • Every deadline dated and linked to its source.

How it plugs into AVA

A matched opportunity arrives as an "AVA starts a conversation" invitation: one push at most, with sources as chips. Unrestricted web crawling is out of scope; this needs its own contract.

Why for you

You're future-focused and you build. Hackathons, fellowships and competitions are the cheapest way to turn a side project into momentum, but they're easy to miss by a day.

Effort: medium · Risk: medium (freshness) · Wow: high
